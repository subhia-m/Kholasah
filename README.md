   ## Finding
### What causes someone to stop learning? 

Intrinsic Motivation & Identity
Loss of a guiding purpose or goal.
A fixed mindset that avoids challenges and fears failure or embarrassment.
Cognitive & Mental Health Barriers
Burnout, chronic stress, cognitive overload, depression, anxiety, and fatigue.
Natural age-related declines in processing speed or memory without compensatory strategies.
Environmental & Structural Factors
Lack of resources, time, money, or access to mentors and opportunities.
Poor instructional design or an unsupportive environment that discourages experimentation.
Behavioral & Habitual Causes
Settling into a comfort zone once a level of "good enough" is reached.
Inconsistent practice, passive consumption, or over-reliance on shortcuts.
Beliefs & Metacognitive Issues
Poor metacognition (failing to accurately assess one's own knowledge).
Unrealistic expectations that lead to discouragement when progress is slow.
External Incentives & Systemic Design
Perverse incentives that reward credentials or status over actual capability.
A "one-time training" culture that treats learning as a finite event rather than continuous practice.

### Why Do Learners Drop Out of Online Courses

Low Completion & Early Exit: Online courses suffer from low completion rates (e.g., MOOC medians around 3% to 12.6%), with many learners dropping out in the first few days or weeks due to low initial commitment or casual enrollment.
Life Balance & Stress: Balancing studies with work and personal responsibilities is a major hurdle. Emotional stress (49%) and mental health concerns (41%) are leading drivers for students considering stopping out.
Financial & Cost Pressures: Cost is a significant deterrent and stop-out factor, especially when learners struggle to see the immediate value or return on investment.
Course Quality & Difficulty: Poor instructional design, unclear expectations, and overly difficult coursework (cited by 24% of students considering stopping out) lead to frustration and abandonment.
Lack of Engagement & Support:
Weak self-regulation (poor time management and effort tracking) reduces persistence.
Absent teaching presence and lack of peer interaction (social isolation) decrease satisfaction and course completion.
Digital distractions (cyberloafing) heavily drive disengagement.
Technology & Accessibility Barriers: A lack of basic digital skills, inaccessible course designs for people with disabilities, and a global digital divide (with billions lacking quality internet access or devices) continue to push learners out of online education.
